# Behavioural Data Science Toolbox - Pressure Cooker 

# Case 
Het Epos Primary School is an educational organisation founded in 2019 by the philanthropic Stichting De Verre Bergen. The school
opened in Rotterdam-Zuid in 2020 and was founded on the conviction that differences in learning outcomes between schools are
related not only to pupils’ backgrounds, but also to the quality of education. The school aspires to be among the best schools in
Rotterdam, with high expectations for language and mathematics as well as broad attention to children’s development into young 
adulthood. 

# Question from Het Epos
Which aspects of early years education should the school prioritise for improvement in order to maximise pupils’ development during
the kindergarten years and their success throughout the rest of their school career?

### Sub-question 1: Extended time in kindergarten
What effects does spending a longer period in kindergarten have on pupils’ cognitive, social-emotional and executive development, and
on their functioning in Years 3 and 4?

### Sub-question 2: Teaching focus aligned with Years 3 and 4
What effects does a teaching focus on learning objectives aligned with Years 3 and 4 have on pupils’ academic achievement and subsequent
educational pathways?
